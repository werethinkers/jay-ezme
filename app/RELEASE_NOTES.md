## Prompt Builder: SeaArt, mic and language

- **SeaArt and similar image/video pages:** the prompt goes into the largest visible prompt box (SeaArt's rich editor), with a fallback typing method for custom editors. The Generate button is found even when its label shows a changing cost such as "Generate (Estimated Cost:161)".
- **Voice:** saying "SeaArt" (or common mishearings such as "see art") now picks the SeaArt link.
- **MacBook mic:** checks that a working microphone exists and explains what to fix if not. Silent gaps no longer end the session with an error.
- **Auto-off:** the mic turns itself off after 10 seconds of silence, with a visible 3-2-1 countdown. Speaking keeps it on.
- **Language:** a language menu next to Speak (English, Hindi, Gujarati, Marathi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Spanish, French, German, Arabic, Japanese).
