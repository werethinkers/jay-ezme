Ezyourlife apps home page, voice-guided forms, and EzMe Home in the browser

- Ezyourlife apps now open on a home page of cards like AppBuilder and WishStudio. Use the ··· on any card to add a cover picture, GIF or video (with crop and position), pick an icon from the gallery, or upload your own icon. Clicking a card opens that app straight away.
- Fill by voice: every app now has a "Fill by voice" button. It goes through the boxes one by one and shows what to say. Say the answer, or say next, back, clear or done. You can also name a box, for example "company name is Astute". Works in English and Hindi, and also fills plain question and picture boxes.
- "Keep in EzMe shortcut" is now always shown next to each box, not only when you hover.
- The full-screen browser has an "EzMe Home" button that leaves full screen and goes to the EzMe home page. The same button is in the browser's top bar.
