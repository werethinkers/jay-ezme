## Prompt Builder: marks added for you, tidier menus, @ for your KeyDo apps, media attaches again

- **Marks are added automatically.** Say or type a name that matches one of your shortcuts, collection records or apps ("rohit media", "priya") and it becomes `#rohit.media` or `@priya` as soon as the word is finished. A "Marked for you" row shows what changed; tap ✕ on one to put the plain word back (it will not be changed again).
- **# Collections now means every shortcut except media**, grouped by collection, A to Z. The first three entries are the shortcuts you made most recently. **# Media** lists the media shortcuts the same way (three recent first, then A to Z).
- **@ is for KeyDo apps**, not Mac apps: your AppBuilder apps, WishStudio apps and Ezyourlife apps. The **@ Apps** menu lists them by group; `@HappyBirthday` writes the app into the prompt.
- **Media attaches again** when you say "enter chatgpt", "enter grok" and the others. Shortcuts said in plain words just before the command are now marked first, and the prompt and its media are worked out fresh at the moment of sending.
