## Cleaner cards and tabs: pictures, GIFs and videos stay in view

- **No more text over the cover.** Hovering an app card in AppBuilder, Ezyourlife apps or WishStudio no longer lays its name and description over the picture, GIF or video, so the cover plays and shows clearly.
- **The details now open in a small popup beside the card or tab:** the name, what it does and what it costs. It appears after a short pause, so it does not flash when you only move across the cards, and it goes away when you move off.
- **Tabs in AppBuilder and WishStudio** show the same popup below them, in place of the plain system tooltip that repeated the words already on the tab.
