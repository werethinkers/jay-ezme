## AppDesigner: faster pickers, app rename that sticks, and 13 ready-made templates

- **Pickers start on the first item.** Boxes that take a wish, a record or media from a collection are filled with the first item to begin with, so the live preview and the app always have something to show. Type or say something else to change it.
- **Type to narrow, nothing loads all at once.** As you type in a wish, record or media box, a short list of the nearest matches appears under it. The Choose list shows only the first few until you type, then the closest ones.
- **Voice takes the best match.** Saying part of a name or a few words from a wish, record or media description picks the closest one, even when the words are only part of it.
- **Renaming an app now saves.** The app name is its own box in the top bar (the box beside it is the page name). Press Return or click away and the app is renamed everywhere. The run word follows the new name, so renaming "new" to "new2" gives \new2-, unless you chose your own word. Saying "rename this app to ..." does the same.
- **13 ready-made templates**, added the first time AppDesigner opens and under Ready layouts any time: Greeting card, Offer letter, Leave application, Product catalogue page, Discount offer, Product ad, Appointment letter (2 pages), Invitation, Save the date with countdown, Certificate of appreciation, Festival countdown story, We are hiring post, and Quotation. They use variables, picture boxes and functions (days to go, days between dates, rupee totals with GST). Change anything by voice, then publish to make it an app.
