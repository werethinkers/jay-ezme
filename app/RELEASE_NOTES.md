Collection Builder cover media and app look

- Collection Builder now has the same look options as the other AppBuilder apps: Icon gallery, Upload icon, Cover media (upload an image, GIF or video, generate with AI, crop and position, preview, remove) and Credits/use.
- Your choices show on its card on the AppBuilder home page and on its tab.
- Collection Builder is a built-in app, so it still cannot be deleted, copied, shared or published to the marketplace. Your own apps keep all of those options.
