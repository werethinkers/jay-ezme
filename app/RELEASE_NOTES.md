## EzMe is now KeyDo

- New name, new logo and a new app icon. KeyDo is shown in the app, the Dock, the menu bar and Finder, and on the website, with the logo on the sidebar, sign-in and account pages.
- The backslash popup's top bar is tidier: one row of matching icons, Options (transparency, category and collection), Resize, Move to default position, New shortcut, then Close. The title stays on one line.
- Your shortcuts, keys, accounts and settings are untouched. The file on your Mac is still called EzMe.app so this update installs over your current copy.
