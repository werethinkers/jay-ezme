## AppDesigner: readable form, templates for every app, and apps that never stop to ask

- **The full form is readable and on brand.** It always uses the light KeyDo look (cream and charcoal with the KeyDo icon), even when the Mac is in dark mode, so the text is no longer white on cream. The same goes for the window that opens from the backslash popup.
- **Typing works after a backslash command.** The popup was still capturing every key when the form opened from it. It now lets go, so you can type in the form straight away. "Open the full form" is always there for design apps.
- **Apps never stop for missing details.** Anything left empty uses the last value you typed in that app, otherwise the app's starting value, otherwise the first item of its collection (first wish, first record, first picture). Press Make, or send the command, with nothing filled and it still works.
- **Required boxes are marked.** A red "* Required" shows on text boxes that have nothing to fall back on, and a grey line shows what an empty box will use. Make never blocks on them; it tells you which were left blank.
- **Every app has at least 3 templates.** temp1 is the default; temp2 and temp3 are made for you as colour and font variations, and you can add more with "Save as new". Names are at most 6 letters and can be renamed. In a command add `-temp2`, `-temp3` or `-tempr` (random), for example `\hbd-rahul-temp2`. The form has a Template menu with the same choices. Publishing releases all templates together. Use "Make default" to swap a template into the default place; temp1 is always the default.
