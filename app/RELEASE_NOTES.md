# KeyDo 4.00

## App Designer, Gifting: a real 3D box
Gifting is now a real 3D object you can turn with the mouse, and look at from the front, back, left, right, top or three-quarter with one tap.

- **A real box:** a base with four walls and a floor, a **hinged lid** that swings open, a clasp, hinges, and the VYBES label on the lid. Same size for every finish: **34 × 24 × 9 cm** (base 7.8 cm, lid 1.2 cm, wall 1.2 cm). Wood, Metal, Leather, Rubber and PU each have their own texture, fittings and lining. These are proposed sizes; send the real ones and they change in one place. (3.99 said 7.8 cm inside depth; the correct clear height with the lid shut is **6.6 cm**.)
- **A tray inside the box**, 1.2 cm thick, with a round **cut-out for each jar**. A jar sits 0.6 cm into the tray, so a jar with its lid can be at most **6.0 cm** tall. The screen shows each jar's diameter and height against its cut-out, and the lid really closes over everything.
- **Real jars, not circles.** Each jar is a turned 3D shape: faceted trinket jar, octagon crystal jar, relief ceramic jar, square perfume bottle, candle bowl, glass jar with wooden lid, reed diffuser bottle and floral tin; in crystal glass, frosted glass, ceramic, metal or wood. Glass is see-through, so you see the **contents inside**: candle, perfume, diffuser, cookies, dry fruits, chocolates, tea, potpourri. Turn the lid off to see them from above.
- **"Your jars, from every side":** every jar of the gift turns slowly on a turntable, with its size.
- **Filling** is 3D in the tray gaps (silk shred, petals, golden beads, pearls, paper), in any colour.
- **Flowers and leaves on the lid**, in real arrangements: Rose garden, Blush blossoms and ferns, Marigold and mango leaves, Lotus and lily, Eucalyptus and pampas, Gold-leaf sprigs and pearls, Satin bow, or a brass monogram plate. A cluster on the lid and a smaller one at the opposite corner.
- **The sender's message:** the card is fixed under the lid and shows when the lid opens. Choose an occasion (Birthday, Anniversary, Thank you, Congratulations, Diwali, Get well, Friendship, Love) and a tone (Heartfelt, Warm and short, Poetic): KeyDo writes the message from the names and what is in the box, and you can edit every word. Say "card to Priya from Rahul, write a heartfelt birthday message".
- **Unboxing** now plays the real thing: the lid swings up, the tray and filling appear, the jars are set in one by one, then the card.
- Views: Closed, Inside, Unboxing, Card, and **Plan** (the top-down drawing with all sizes). Save picture now saves the 3D view, the plan and the card.
- New voice: "front view", "back view", "top view", "open the lid", "close the lid", "jar 2 lid off", "show plan".
- Prices are still placeholders (₹3,000 to ₹10,000).

## Keyboard safety
Checked: nothing in the key tap `miniJSlashTap`, the popup, voice key capture or any key-swallowing mode (`pickerActive`, `imagePromptMode`, `triggerTracking`) was changed. The change is only in the WishStudio Gifting page.
