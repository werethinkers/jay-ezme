**Prompt Builder voice**
- Fixes words being typed twice while you speak: the same finished sentence arriving twice is ignored, and words the speech engine repeats at the start of a new round are dropped.
- **Said twice** fix: in the Check the prompt box, a word or phrase said twice in a row becomes one, live as you type or speak (for example "send it to him send it to him"). It follows the Auto-correct switch, and Polish with AI also removes repeats. Shortcuts, names, formulas and attachment labels are never touched.
- "Send to ChatGPT", "enter ChatGPT", "share with Grok", "paste in Claude" and the same for your other sites are steadier: a spoken send waits until you stop speaking (about a second), so it never sends half a prompt, and names heard as separate letters ("chat gee pee tee") are understood.

**Keyboard safety:** checked. The key tap, popup and every mode that swallows keys are untouched.
