## More ways to make shortcuts from contacts and MyDatabase

- New code styles from a person's name: full first name + first letter of surname (for example yogeshn), first 3 letters + first letter of surname, full first name + first 2 letters of surname, surname + first letter of first name, and surname only. They appear in MyDatabase and in Collection Builder's contact import.
- Names with a | sign (like "Yogesh Nagar | ABC Corp | Indore") now use only the part on the left of the |. This applies to VCF and CSV imports. Contacts already saved in MyDatabase with a | are tidied the next time the app opens, and the text after the | moves into their notes.
- Two new shortcuts per contact: a full contact card (name, company, phone, email, address) and a WhatsApp link made from the mobile number.
- Links and files can now take their code from the full name, the first 3 letters of each word, the initials, the first word only, or, for links, the website name.
