# KeyDo 3.93

## WishStudio: new Gifting section (VYBES)
- A new "Gifting" card on the WishStudio home, next to Infographics.
- 30 gift boxes of the same size, priced from Rs 3,000 to Rs 10,000, each with its own colours, a decoration on the lid and up to 6 items (diffusers, perfumes, candles, eatables, gift items). Search by name or item, or filter by price.
- One spoken or typed sentence changes everything at once, for example "make it burgundy with a gold ribbon and ivory tissue, add perfume and a diffuser, put flowers on top". Also: remove or replace items, "box 12", "next box", "cheaper", "under 5k", quantity, "undo", "reset".
- The colours, decorations and the full item catalogue stay on screen beside the box, so every change can also be made by tapping.
- Four views: Closed, Inside (how each item sits in the box), Unboxing (ribbon off, lid up, tissue parts, items appear one by one, card tucked in) and Card. Say "show inside", "unbox", "show card".
- The VYBES logo is on the box, under the lid, on the card, in the picture you can save and on every order.
- Customisable card (to, from, message, four styles) and a gift experience note, with a suggested note you can replace ("note ...").
- Order: name, phone, address and quantity by voice or by typing ("order name Asha, phone 9876543210, address ..., place order"). The order is saved on the Mac, its summary is copied, and it can be sent on WhatsApp or by email. Payment and delivery are not connected.
- A gift always stays between Rs 3,000 and Rs 10,000: an item that would break the range is refused with a message.
- "Save picture to Desktop" makes one image of the closed box, the inside and the card.
- The Mac smoke test now checks that VybesLogo.png is in the app.

## Keyboard safety
Checked: nothing in the key tap `miniJSlashTap`, the popup, voice or any key-swallowing mode was changed. Gifting is a normal WishStudio page and its commands come from the existing WishStudio command bar. The release guard and the heartbeat watchdog were not touched.
