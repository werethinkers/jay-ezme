## Voice that listens, then rests

- **Auto-off:** after 10 seconds with nothing said, the popup and app mic turn off. The last 3 seconds show a countdown ("Turning off in 3…"), and speaking keeps the mic on. Background listening, if you turned it on in voice settings, keeps running.
- **Say "KeyDo":** while the mic is listening in the background, say "KeyDo" (or "Hey KeyDo") to open the popup.
- **Apps by sentence:** Send, Create, Make, Generate, Write and similar words start an app, for example "Send Diwali greetings to Mohit". More starter words were added.
- **Collections and shortcuts by voice:** say "hashtag birthday" or "hash media files rahul" to pick a collection and search inside it, "run collection X", "run shortcut X" or "run X" to find and paste a shortcut, "insert function date" (or "insert date") to paste a live value, and "all collections" to search everywhere again.
