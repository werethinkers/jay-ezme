## AppDesigner: category and subcategory only, and a command language you choose

- **Library is two levels now.** Pick a category, then a subcategory (a collection such as HBD Wish), then press Add. The person picks the exact shortcut, name or media when the app runs, so templates no longer link down to individual shortcuts or items. If you select a box first, the subcategory goes into that box. For a list of people or companies you can also choose which field to show, such as the name or email.
- **The right-hand fields are named after the subcategory.** A box that takes its choice from a subcategory is labelled with that subcategory ("HBD Wish") and shows its category next to it, instead of a generic variable name.
- **Choose the language of voice commands.** The command bar has an English | हिन्दी switch. It is English every time the designer opens, whatever the rest of the app is set to. If you pick Hindi, command words and numbers are still understood.
- **The whole page answers to voice.** What you say to the command mic that is not a design change now fills the live preview: "name Rahul wish ss10" fills those boxes, and "make it" makes the file.
