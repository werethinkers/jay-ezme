## AppDesigner: voice that understands you, media by description, and example commands

- **Voice commands work when the mic hears Hindi script.** The designer mic was writing English command words in Devanagari ("टेक्स्ट बॉक्स", "मेक इट") and every one failed with "I did not understand". The command mic now listens in English (India), and anything that still comes back in Devanagari is turned into the English command first. Common Hindi command words work too: जोड़ो (add), हटाओ (delete), बड़ा (bigger), colours, page numbers and so on.
- **Collections of media work like collections of wishes.** In a picture box, type or say the media's name or words from its description ("birthday cake") and the matching media appears with a small picture to tap. Press Return to take the best match. Fill by voice does the same.
- **A media collection becomes a picture box.** Choosing a collection of pictures or videos from Dropdown adds a picture box named after it, with a Choose button that lists only that collection and searches by name or description. The shortcuts picker now also searches descriptions.
- **Say or type it like this.** Under the command word in the live preview, KeyDo now suggests the full sentence, the short sentence and the typed command for your app, built from its own boxes, for example "Make New design 2 for Rahul with wish advent". Tap a line to copy it.
