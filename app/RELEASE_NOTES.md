# KeyDo 3.63

- **Rename an app any time.** Every app card in App Designer has a pencil next to its name: type the new name and press Save. Inside the designer the app name now has a visible "Save name" button. Released apps keep working: the backslash popup and its run word follow the new name (unless you set your own run word).
- **Ask AI to design it.** In the New App wizard, AI now designs the page instead of only moving boxes: a big confident title, quieter details, grouped boxes, whitespace, and one small colour palette chosen to read well on your page or uploaded background. It also picks a font, text size, colour, bold or italic, alignment and a coloured band for each box. You can still change any box afterwards.
- **From a reference.** In step 2, "From a reference" takes a picture or PDF of an app, form, invoice or card you like. AI reads it and adds the boxes that change each time as variables (with the right data type and an example value) and the words that never change as fixed text, laid out and styled like the reference, and suggests the app name. Needs an AI key in Setup.
