# KeyDo 3.62

- **Fixed text and fixed media.** In the New App wizard, a variable, collection or picture that has a ready value can be made fixed: it is printed as is and the form never asks for it. Pictures can be a fixed file. In the designer, "Turn variables here into fixed text" (or "Make this picture fixed") on any box swaps the variable for its ready value.
- **Where you are.** New functions CITY, PLACE (city, state, country), REGION, COUNTRY and COORDS. The place is found from your internet address, so it is accurate to the city and sends your address to a free lookup service (ipwho.is).
- **More live functions from free sources.** TEMPERATURE, WEATHER, HUMIDITY, WIND, SUNRISE, SUNSET, UV, AQI (Open-Meteo), RATE("USD","INR") (Frankfurter), CRYPTO("bitcoin","inr") (CoinGecko), QUOTE (quotes from ZenQuotes.io), and MOON, DAYOFYEAR, YEARLEFT which work offline. They appear under "Live & place" in the function list and work as =City, =Weather and so on. Values refresh in the background when the app starts or a form opens; the first use may show a dash until they arrive.
