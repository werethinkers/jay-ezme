# KeyDo 3.79

- **Settings → Words shortcuts cannot be:** the full list of words that would clash with voice and commands, now over 200: action words (send, play, call, show, make, design, create, generate, open, write, search, …), pronouns and small words. Add your own, remove any (click the x), search the list, reset to the standard list, or switch the check off. New shortcuts, edited shortcuts and bulk pastes all follow this list, and KeyDo suggests a suffix such as .pic, .phone or .mobile.
- **Settings → Siri & voice commands:** switch on "Use Siri as a voice command across all apps". KeyDo then accepts keydo:// links from Siri and the Shortcuts app, so from any app you can say "Hey Siri, KeyDo" and a command such as "open prompt builder" or "send to chatgpt". The settings page has the one-time Shortcuts setup and copyable links.
- Keyboard safety: no change to the key watcher.
