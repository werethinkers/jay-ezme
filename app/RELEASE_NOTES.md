# KeyDo 4.64

**Every Ez Tool has its own cover: picture, GIF or video**
- On the Ez Tools Home page each card has a picture button. Press it to choose an image, GIF or video for that tool only.
- Each tool page now opens with its own banner showing that tool's cover, with a **Change cover** button. Changing one tool no longer changes the others.
- The Home page keeps its own cover under the header.
- Tool tabs show a picture or GIF cover as their icon. A video cover shows on the card and the banner, and the tab keeps its coloured icon.

**Keyboard safety:** checked. The key tap, popup, voice and every mode that swallows keys are untouched.
