# KeyDo 3.94

## Gifting rebuilt around real product photos
- The cartoon boxes are gone. Gifting now uses 63 real product photographs cropped from the four Izzhaar Diwali catalogues (under 1,000, 1,500, 2,000 and 2,500), bundled in the app.
- 30 hampers (₹3,000 to ₹9,500), each built from those products and always the same size: one landscape VYBES box, shown from above with the products laid out in a 3 by 2 grid under the VYBES plate, with the card tucked in beside it.
- Views: Closed (the lid from above with ribbon, gold studs, VYBES plate and decoration), Inside (the products laid out, named), Unboxing (ribbon off, lid lifts, tissue parts, products appear one by one, card) and Card.
- The VYBES logo is on the lid, inside the box, on the card, in the saved picture and on every order.
- Voice works on everything: change colours ("royal blue box, silver ribbon, ivory tissue"), add or remove products by name ("add the coconut candle and the serenade tea collection") or by kind ("add a frame", "remove the candles"), browse by kind ("show candles", "show trays"), switch box ("box 12", "next box", "under 5k"), decorations, quantity, card to/from/message, gift note, "show inside", "unbox", "place order", "undo".
- The product catalogue with photos stays beside the box while you customise. Tap a photo to add or remove it.
- A gift stays between ₹3,000 and ₹10,000 (products plus a ₹500 box fee; extra decorations ₹200 each).
- Item prices are the catalogue price bands (₹1,000, ₹1,500, ₹2,000, ₹2,500) until exact prices are entered.
- Orders are saved on the Mac, copied, and can be sent on WhatsApp or email. Payment and delivery are not connected.
- The Mac smoke test now checks that the product photos are in the app.

## Keyboard safety
Checked: nothing in the key tap `miniJSlashTap`, the popup, voice or any key-swallowing mode was changed. Gifting is a normal WishStudio page driven by the existing command bar. The release guard and the heartbeat watchdog were not touched.
