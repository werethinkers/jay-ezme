## New app: a three-step guide, plus say-what-you-need creation

- **New app button in AppDesigner.** Step 1: pick Invoice, Birthday wish, Visiting card, Invitation, Quotation or Blank, or upload your own background. A logo spot is already on the page (top left, top centre or top right to suit the kind). Click the logo to move it, resize it, pick a style or upload your own logo.
- **Step 2: add your boxes.** Variables (with a data type), collections, functions, pictures and text. Add them from the menu, with "Add the usual boxes" for the kind of page, or just say or type it: "I need two variables string and date, two collections, one picture and a countdown function". The page on the right changes as you go.
- **Collections ask the right questions.** Each collection box asks for its category, then the subcategory, then a default shortcut or media, which is used whenever nothing is chosen.
- **Ask AI to place them.** One button asks your AI model to arrange the boxes so they do not overlap and read well; Tidy does the same in plain order, and is used when no AI key is set.
- **Step 3: name it.** Set the app name and the word that runs it from the backslash popup, see how many variables, collections, functions, pictures and existing designs you have, and tick which boxes are **mandatory**. A mandatory box shows a red "Required", and the full form and the popup will not make anything until it is filled. Everything else still falls back to the last or a ready value.
- New blank pages made from the Blank menu now also start with a logo spot.
