# KeyDo 4.55

**Database Organizer is now My Files, with Finder built in**
- New **Finder** tab (opens first): Desktop, Documents, Downloads, Pictures, other drives and any folder you add under My folders. Drag a folder onto the sidebar to pin it.
- Back, forward, enclosing folder, a clickable path bar, Go to folder, list or icon view, search and sort.
- Drag files and folders in from your Desktop or Finder, or between folders, to move them (Option copies). Dragging to another drive copies.
- New folder, Rename, Duplicate, Copy, Cut, Paste, Move to, Copy to, Compress, Quick Look, Get info, Reveal in Finder and Move to Trash (recoverable).
- Add to My Files library saves a file so you can tag it, add notes and search inside it. PDF and Word files can open in the KeyDo viewer.
- The Organizer, Recent Files, Contacts, Links, Files, Media, Tables, Shortcuts and File Renamer tabs are unchanged.

**One-row top menu**
- The two rows of links are now one stylish row. Click a group (Library, Create, Apps & web, Setup) to open a menu with an icon and a short line for every page. The page you are on shows next to its group.

**Home: your apps and links together**
- Your apps and links shows AppDesigner, AppBuilder, WishStudio and Ezyourlife apps plus your saved links, with colourful chips to filter them. Tap one to open it; WishStudio opens that exact app.

**Keyboard safety:** checked. The key tap, popup, voice and every mode that swallows keys are untouched.
