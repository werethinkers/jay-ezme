## The popup closes itself when you leave it idle

- **Open the backslash popup and do nothing?** After 10 seconds, with nothing typed and nothing said, a small 3-2-1 count shows on the right side of the popup and then it closes.
- **Typing, speaking, moving the pointer over the popup, scrolling or dragging it keeps it open** and starts the wait again. A popup with text in it, or one doing voice, image or rewrite work, is never closed this way.
