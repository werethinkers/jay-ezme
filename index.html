How it works

Three keystrokes between you and done.

No window to open and no app to switch to. EzMe waits in the background until you type a backslash.

1

Type a backslash

In any text field, type \ followed by the start of a shortcut key. A popup lists everything that matches.

\inv
2

Pick, or finish the key

Choose a result, or type the full key and press Space. Missing details are collected in a short popup.

Space
3

It expands in place

Text, live values, calculations, attached media or an AI-generated draft land where your cursor is.

Done
Highlights

A small utility with a very long reach.

Six things people notice first.

Collections that organise themselves

Group shortcuts into built-in or custom collections. Applications, AI engines, websites and media each add their own suffix to the key, so \chrome.app launches an app and \logo.media pastes a file.

\chrome.app\copilot.ai\news.web\logo.media\laugh.gif\smile.e

Live values that stay current

Drop a token into any phrase and it fills in at the moment you expand it.

{{date}}{{time}}{{datetime}}{{weekday}}{{uuid}}{{clipboard}}{{worldclock:Tokyo}}

A calculator in the popup

Type an expression after the backslash. The result shows in the popup and pastes when you press Space. Percentages, powers, parentheses and common functions work.

\45+4590
\(12+8)*360
\sqrt(81)9

Two AI builders, your choice of model

Collection Builder researches a topic with web search and returns ready shortcuts with source links. AppBuilder makes reusable actions that combine your saved records, information, calculations and a selected layout into finished output.

OpenAIGoogle GeminiAnthropic ClaudeOpenAI-compatible

A browser that knows your shortcuts

EzMe Browser has tabs, named tab groups, favourites and a start page. Import Chrome bookmarks, turn on a magnifier lens for fine detail, and lock the whole workspace behind a six-digit Private Mode passcode.

Ez Studio for images and video

Generate images, resize to an exact aspect ratio, trim video, rename files in bulk, or crop any part of your screen and save it straight in as a shortcut.

Documents and writing

From saved records to a finished page.

Keep customers, products, candidates or contacts as collections. Pick records, fill the gaps, and EzMe builds the document from your template. Totals and GST can be calculated on your Mac with no AI call at all.

Media shortcuts

Privacy and permissions

Asks only for what a feature needs.

macOS will prompt for each permission the first time a related feature is used. Text stays on your Mac unless you paste it into another app or send it to an AI provider you configured.

Accessibility

Lets the popup find the app you were typing in and enter text there.

Automation

Expands shortcuts inside other apps.

Screen recording

Captures only the area you select when you choose Crop screen.

Microphone

Used only when you pick voice input for prompts and details.

Speech recognition

Turns that voice input into text.
